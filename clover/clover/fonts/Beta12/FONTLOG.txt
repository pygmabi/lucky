Basic Font Information

Cancelleresca Bastarda, designed by Jan van Krimpen in 1934, is possibly the one of the finest typeface in the world.

Inspired by Writing Masters of the 15th Century (such as Casanova, Tagliente, Arrighi and Palatino) Jan van Krimpen Cancelleresca Bastarda brings the Chancery genre to a new level.

We are making a digital revival, trying to keep it as close to the original as possible.
At this stage, we are desperately looking for good quality source images.

This is a very early beta release.
At this stage our main focus is into getting the lowercase letter-shapes as close to the originals as possible.
